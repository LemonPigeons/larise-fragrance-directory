# Independent Fragrance Equivalent Directory

> An objective, community-curated reference guide comparing L’ARISÉ fragrance numbers with their corresponding designer and niche scent profiles.

## Dataset Overview
- **Total Entries:** 218 fragrance comparisons
- **Women's Collection (002–201):** 149 fragrances
- **Men's Collection (406–499):** 59 fragrances
- **Niche & Unisex Collection (700–709):** 10 fragrances
