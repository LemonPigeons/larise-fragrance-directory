A Few Quick Notes

How do the L’ARISÉ fragrance numbers work?

The numbering follows a simple split: 002–121 are the women's scents, 402–470 are the men's scents, and 700–740 cover the niche and unisex inspired releases.

Are these exact 1-to-1 copies of the original perfumes?

They are inspired equivalents, meaning they share the same olfactory family and core note structure as the designer or niche originals. As with any inspired fragrance, projection and dry-down can vary slightly from bottle to bottle.

Is this an official L’ARISÉ website?

No. This is an independent reference page put together for easy comparison. All designer and niche brand names belong to their respective owners.
