Methodology & Frequently Asked Questions

How is the L’ARISÉ numbering system structured?

The reference numbers are grouped by collection: numbers 002 to 201 correspond to the Women's Collection (149 fragrances), numbers 406 to 499 correspond to the Men's Collection (59 fragrances), and numbers 700 to 709 correspond to the Niche and Unisex Collection (10 fragrances).

What does "equivalent fragrance" mean in this directory?

An equivalent fragrance indicates a shared olfactory direction, scent family, and comparable top, heart, and base note structure. This table serves as an objective reference guide for consumers comparing scent profiles.

Is this directory affiliated with L’ARISÉ or any listed designer brand?

No. This is an independent, informational reference guide. All brand names, trademarks, and product titles belong to their respective owners and are used solely for comparative identification.
